Live device

Tap the face to press, tap twice quickly for double press, press and hold to choose. Keep holding for 8 s to pair headphones. Track, playlist and headphone names on this page are examples.

42 mm

Space acts as the face button: tap, tap twice, or hold. Shortcuts anywhere: P press, D double press, H hold, 8 hold 8 seconds, W wrist up or down.

    Interaction map

    Double press walks the three main screens. Press acts on the screen you are on. Hold chooses or confirms; its path is drawn in the LED colour because the LEDs fill while you hold.

    Screen inventory

    Every state the screen can be in, drawn by the same renderer as the live pod. The small id under each tile is its PNG file name.

    Type, icons, LED language

    One 5 × 7 face, hand set dot by dot. Digits are all 5 dots wide so numbers do not jump while they change. Big numbers are the same face at 2× with 1 dot tracking, 14 of the 16 rows. A 3 × 5 face carries labels and secondary lines.

    CountA level. Battery, track progress, pace guide, list position.
    Moving lightWorking. GPS search, pairing search.
    BlinkPaused, or the sync stage now running.
    FlashAn event. Each km, headphones found, headphones lost.
    FillingYou are holding. Full means it is done.

    Track, playlist and headphone names, the firmware number and the run figures are examples. The run is simulated: pace drifts around a 5:10 /km target.

    Screen PNGs for the portfolio are in png/ (amber) and png-white/, one per inventory id, 8 px per dot, transparent, smoked overlay on. Each file is 544 × 224 px: a 16 px margin, the screen at 16, 16 (512 × 128), the LEDs centred 44 px below the screen.
