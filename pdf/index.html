    ABHIKGUPTACortX
    ABHIKGUPTACortX · brief

    Until now, we've only measured the body. Now it's time to measure the mind.

    Clubs track almost everything about a player's body and nothing about their mind. Mental fatigue hurts decisions before it shows up physically, and the tools that could see it are wired lab caps nobody can sprint in.

    01 HardwareBrain-sensing kit is heavy, wired and slow to put on.
    02 TrustCoaches trust the eye test over a new kind of data.
    03 FrictionIt needs outside specialists, adding cost and delay.
    For
    The lead data analyst and head coach at a top club
    Reads
    EEG, fNIRS, head motion, heart rate, GPS
    abkgpta.github.io/portfolio27abhikthegupta@gmail.com
    ABHIKGUPTACortX · research

    Research

    Research ran from liveable spaces, through neurodiversity and sensory-centred design, to Sensory Intelligence Optimisation. We studied the Red Bull Athlete Performance Centre and P3, then built a fit mock-up of the liner in plastic straps and foam.

    Brain-imaging headsetTaken forward.
    Blind-spot wristbandHaptic cue when someone enters a blind spot.
    Game disruptorA robot that breaks a player's rhythm.
    abkgpta.github.io/portfolio27abhikthegupta@gmail.com
    ABHIKGUPTACortX · the system

    The system

    1. HeadsetDry EEG and fNIRS in a light shell. One Boa dial fits many heads.
    2. Back unitAmplifier, battery, GPS, Bluetooth. An egg-shaped 52 × 92 × 24 mm unit in a vest plate, on a magnetic cable.
    3. Charging dockA bench station for a squad of 25.
    4. Pitch-side appLive focus, load and fatigue per player on a tablet.
    5. Analyst dashboardSessions over time, with Catapult and STATSports data.

    CortX assists the coach's judgement. It never overrides it.

    abkgpta.github.io/portfolio27abhikthegupta@gmail.com
    ABHIKGUPTACortX · inside
    EEGfNIRSIMUHRGPS

    EEG neurofeedback training improved sport motor performance with a large effect (Hedges' g 0.78) across 21 randomised trials. Yu et al. 2025, Scand J Med Sci Sports.

    abkgpta.github.io/portfolio27abhikthegupta@gmail.com
    ABHIKGUPTACortX · a day at the club

    A day at the club

    1. Assess · morningHeadset and vest on. A short baseline sets today's starting point.
    2. Train · sessionFocus, load and fatigue stream to the coach's tablet, live.
    3. Profile · afternoonThe analyst joins it with GPS and event data.
    4. Prepare · matchdaySelection uses what the week showed. The coach makes the call.
