    cortX02 · Design and Technology
    ABHIKGUPTAcortX
    ABHIKGUPTAcortX · brief

    You can only improve what you measure.

    A brain-imaging headset for elite football training.

    Top training facilities measure almost everything about a player's body, but have nothing for kinaesthetic and spatial awareness. cortX is worn during training and collects positional and neurological data for a club's analysts.

    For
    Lead data analyst, Premier League club
    Team
    Abhik Gupta, Ansha Gawande
    abkgpta.github.io/portfolio27abhikthegupta@gmail.com
    ABHIKGUPTAcortX · what it reads

    Four sensors, two units.

    The headset carries the brain and head sensors: EEG, fNIRS and IMU. A vest unit holds the microcontroller, analog front end, heart rate monitor, GPS, Bluetooth and battery, and sends everything to the analysts' computers.

    abkgpta.github.io/portfolio27abhikthegupta@gmail.com
